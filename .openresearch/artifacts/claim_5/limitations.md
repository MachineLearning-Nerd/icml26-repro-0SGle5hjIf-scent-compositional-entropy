# Claim 5 limitations and deviations

No performance experiment is reported for Claim 5. `BLOCKED` is not a PASS and
does not support or contradict the paper's ordering. The public raw images are
available, but the paper-specific extracted features are not, and recreating
them plus the full factorial training protocol is outside the authorized
CPU-only host's storage and practical work capacity.

The public-asset inventory is time-stamped because authors may publish features
later. If exact extracted tensors become available, this blocker should be
revisited rather than retained by default.
